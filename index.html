A Heart for God. A Heart for People.

Charity International is founded on a simple conviction: loving God and loving our neighbors belong together.

Throughout Scripture, we see God’s concern for the poor, the vulnerable, the widow, the orphan, and the stranger. We see Jesus approach people who were overlooked, respond to suffering with compassion, and call His followers to do the same.

That calling is the foundation of our mission. We believe people deserve to be seen as more than their circumstances. Not as statistics or problems to solve, but as individuals made in the image of God, worthy of love, dignity, and care.

Love Your Neighbor. Without Exception.

In Luke 10, Jesus tells the story of the Good Samaritan.

He doesn’t begin by asking where the injured man came from, what he believes, or what he can offer in return. He sees a person in need. And he responds with mercy.

At Charity International, we believe compassion should reach beyond borders, backgrounds, and circumstances. Our faith is the reason we serve. Our hope is that our service reflects the love of Christ.

Faith in Action

James 2:14–17

Our service flows from our faith in Jesus Christ. We believe genuine faith should produce love, generosity, humility, and concern for others.

Dignity for Every Person

Genesis 1:27

Every human being is created in the image of God. We believe people should be treated with respect and compassion regardless of their circumstances.

Compassion Without Borders

Luke 10:25–37

Suffering is not limited by geography. Our responsibility to love our neighbors extends beyond the people and communities most familiar to us.

Responsible Stewardship

1 Peter 4:10

Generosity is a gift of trust. We believe charitable resources should be handled with honesty, wisdom, accountability, and care.

Service With Humility

Philippians 2:3–4

We believe meaningful humanitarian work requires listening, learning, and working alongside people rather than assuming we know what they need.

Our Areas of Focus

Compassion Begins With Listening. It Grows Through Action.

Recognize the Need

From hunger and poverty to displacement and unexpected hardship, we seek to understand where practical assistance is needed and how we can responsibly contribute.

Work Together

We believe effective humanitarian service is strengthened through trusted relationships—with churches, community organizations, local leaders, and people who understand the challenges their communities face.

Serve With Purpose

Our aim is to support compassionate initiatives that address meaningful needs while respecting the dignity, circumstances, and long-term well-being of the people they serve.

Compassion Deserves Integrity.

When someone chooses to give, they are placing trust in a mission they believe matters.

We take that responsibility seriously. As our outreach develops, we want to build a culture of responsible stewardship, thoughtful decision-making, and honest communication.

That means being clear about what we’re working toward, sharing verified updates when initiatives are underway, and recognizing that every contribution deserves careful consideration.
